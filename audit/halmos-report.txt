Symbolic execution covers ALL possible inputs in the specified preconditions
(via Z3 SMT solver). Unlike fuzz testing which samples random concrete inputs,
Halmos provides mathematical proof of invariants over the entire input space.

The `revert TotalSupplyMismatch()` defensive branch in the constructor was not
included as a check function because it is unreachable by construction:
the six allocation constants sum to MAX_SUPPLY exactly.

=======================================================
LIMITATIONS / SCOPE NOTES
=======================================================

- Permit (EIP-2612) signature verification is not in the symbolic execution scope
  because it involves cryptographic ecrecover operations that Halmos models as
  uninterpreted functions. Permit semantics are covered by concrete Foundry
  tests including replay-attack reverts (test/Arxia.t.sol).

- The invariants check the BEHAVIOR of the contract's public surface. They do
  not prove the ABSENCE of side channels (e.g., gas griefing) but the contract
  has no gas-relevant control flow beyond standard OZ ERC20 operations.

- Verification is single-transaction. Multi-transaction sequence invariants
  are covered by Foundry's invariant testing (test/Arxia.t.sol line 459).
